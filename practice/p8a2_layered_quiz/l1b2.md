5. What is chunking, and why split documents into chunks before embedding them?

   **A:** Chunking is a step in the RAG process where the given corpus (set of documents, data, text) is, per item, split into sub-items - "chunks" and then an embedding model is used for each chunk to create a vector for that chunk. chunks are used both for structure - since documents can vary greatly in length, and for relevance, since a single document could cover many topics, individual pieces of it are more likely to be semantically more isolated.

6. What is compaction, and what's one risk of it?

   **A:** Compaction is used to compress the context of a long conversation/prompt, to stay within the context window. the risk is information loss, like any compression system.
